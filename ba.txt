2. INDIAN TAX SYSTEM — KEY CONCEPTS TO COVER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

REGIME SYSTEM (New in 2020, default from FY 2023-24):
  Old Regime: Higher tax rates, allows all deductions (80C, HRA, 80D, etc.)
  New Regime: Lower tax rates, most deductions removed, ₹75,000 std deduction
  Key user question: "Which regime saves me more?" → #1 tool to build

TAX SLABS (FY 2025-26):
  New Regime (default):
    0 – ₹4L:       NIL
    ₹4L – ₹8L:     5%
    ₹8L – ₹12L:    10%
    ₹12L – ₹16L:   15%
    ₹16L – ₹20L:   20%
    ₹20L – ₹24L:   25%
    Above ₹24L:     30%
    + 4% Health & Education Cess on tax
    + Surcharge (10% if >₹50L, 15% if >₹1Cr, 25% if >₹2Cr, 37% if >₹5Cr)
    Rebate u/s 87A: ₹60,000 for income up to ₹12L (effectively 0 tax)

  Old Regime:
    0 – ₹2.5L:     NIL
    ₹2.5L – ₹5L:   5%
    ₹5L – ₹10L:    20%
    Above ₹10L:     30%
    + 4% Cess + Surcharge
    Rebate u/s 87A: ₹12,500 for income up to ₹5L

FINANCIAL YEAR: April 1 – March 31
ASSESSMENT YEAR: The year after FY (FY 2025-26 = AY 2026-27)

KEY DEDUCTIONS (Old Regime only unless noted):
  Section 80C:  ₹1,50,000 limit — PPF, ELSS, LIC, EPF, home loan principal,
                Sukanya, NSC, 5yr FD, tuition fees, SCSS
  Section 80D:  Health insurance premium — ₹25,000 (self) + ₹25,000 (parents)
                Senior citizen parents: ₹50,000 limit
  Section 80CCD(1B): NPS additional ₹50,000 (over 80C limit)
  Section 80CCD(2): Employer NPS contribution (allowed in New Regime too)
  Section 24(b): Home loan interest — ₹2,00,000 (self-occupied)
  HRA:          House Rent Allowance exemption (salaried)
                = min of: actual HRA received, 50%/40% of basic (metro/non-metro),
                  actual rent paid – 10% of basic
  Standard Deduction: ₹75,000 (New Regime) / ₹50,000 (Old Regime) for salaried
  Section 80E:  Education loan interest (no limit)
  Section 80G:  Charitable donations (50%–100% deduction)
  Section 80TTA/80TTB: Savings/FD interest deduction
  Section 80EEA: Additional home loan interest ₹1,50,000 (affordable housing)

TDS (Tax Deducted at Source):
  Employer deducts TDS from salary monthly
  Form 16: TDS certificate from employer (issued June each year)
  Form 26AS / AIS: tax credit statement (reconcile before filing)
  TDS on: rent (>₹50,000/mo), professional fees, FD interest, etc.

ADVANCE TAX (if tax liability > ₹10,000):
  15 Jun: 15% of annual tax
  15 Sep: 45% of annual tax
  15 Dec: 75% of annual tax
  15 Mar: 100% of annual tax

ITR FORMS:
  ITR-1 (Sahaj): Salaried, one house, income < ₹50L
  ITR-2:         Capital gains, multiple properties, foreign income
  ITR-3:         Business/profession income
  ITR-4 (Sugam): Presumptive taxation (44AD/44ADA/44AE)

PRESUMPTIVE TAXATION:
  44AD:  Business — 8%/6% of turnover (limit ₹3Cr)
  44ADA: Professionals (CA, doctor, etc.) — 50% of gross receipts (limit ₹75L)

CAPITAL GAINS (post Budget 2024):
  STCG on equity/MF: 20% (held < 12 months)
  LTCG on equity/MF: 12.5% above ₹1,25,000 (held > 12 months)
  STCG on property: Slab rate (held < 24 months)
  LTCG on property: 12.5% without indexation (held > 24 months)
  STCG on debt MF: Slab rate (always, post April 2023)

GST:
  Rates: 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%
  Composition scheme: 1%/2%/6% on turnover
  Registration mandatory: turnover > ₹40L (goods) / ₹20L (services)
  GSTR filings: GSTR-1 (monthly/quarterly), GSTR-3B (monthly)
